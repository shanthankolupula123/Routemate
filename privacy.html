🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026 | Routemate Telangana Platform

1. Information We Collect

When you use Routemate, we collect only the necessary information to provide secure carpooling services:

  • Account Details: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, Verified Email Address (via Google OAuth or OTP), and optional Gender/Date of Birth.
  • Driver Documents (KYC): Driving License (DL), Vehicle Registration (RC), and identity proof photos strictly used for verification.
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates collected only when actively searching for rides or sharing live trips.
  • Payment Identifiers: Personal UPI ID (e.g. yourname@okhdfcbank) and optional UPI QR code to facilitate direct rider-to-driver settlements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to:

  • Match commuters travelling along identical corridors across Telangana.
  • Enable direct P2P UPI payments between riders and drivers with 0% platform fee.
  • Facilitate Emergency SOS assistance (police helpline 112) and WhatsApp trip sharing.
  • Verify driver identity and roadworthiness through Super Admin review.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party advertisers.

3. Location Privacy & Permissions

Location access is only utilized during active ride creation, pickup search, and navigation. We do not continuously track your device when the application is idle or closed.

4. Secure Document Handling & Super Admin Access

Driver verification documents (DL and RC) are accessible exclusively to authorized compliance management (shanthankolupula123@gmail.com) for manual verification and are never made public to co-passengers.

5. Right to Erasure & Account Deletion (DPDP Compliance)

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India, you retain absolute ownership of your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly through the "Delete Account & Data" option in your Profile.
