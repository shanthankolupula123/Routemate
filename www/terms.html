📜 Official Policy

Terms of Service & Commute Agreement

Last Updated: November 10, 2026 | Routemate Telangana Platform

1. Nature of Platform: Peer-to-Peer Cost Sharing (Non-Commercial)

Routemate is a peer-to-peer technology platform that connects individual commuters travelling along identical corridors across Telangana for voluntary cost-sharing. Routemate is not a taxi aggregator, motor cab service, or commercial fleet operator.

Fares collected represent voluntary sharing of fuel, toll, and vehicle maintenance expenses between fellow commuters.

2. ₹0 Platform Commission on Commuter Fares

Routemate collects 0% platform commission from commuters on ride fares. 100% of the rider contribution goes directly from the commuter to the driver via Direct Peer-to-Peer (P2P) UPI or cash upon ride completion.

3. Driver Requirements & Mandatory KYC Verification

Every driver offering commute seats must submit mandatory Know Your Customer (KYC) documentation:

  • Valid Indian Driving License (DL).
  • Valid Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC).
  • Valid Government Identity Proof (Aadhaar).
  • Clear profile photo and registered vehicle details.

All submissions are securely reviewed by our compliance administration (shanthankolupula123@gmail.com) before vehicle activation.

4. Passenger Safety & Zero Tolerance Standards

Routemate enforces a strict Zero Tolerance policy regarding harassment, rash driving, reckless behavior, or substance abuse.

An integrated Emergency SOS button with 1-tap connection to local police helplines (112) and real-time WhatsApp trip sharing is accessible during all active trips. Violating drivers face permanent suspension.

5. User Conduct & Account Termination

Users agree to provide true and accurate information during registration. Routemate reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate community safety rules or attempt fraudulent activities.
